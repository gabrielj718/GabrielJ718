# GB Media Co. Agency: Fiverr + Upwork launch kit

Draft copy. Prices are starting suggestions; adjust to your capacity. Do not add fake reviews, client names, or results you have not delivered.

## Which first?

**Fiverr first.** Buyers browse fixed-price packages, so a productized SEO audit sells without proposals. You can be live in a day.
**Upwork second.** Better for retainers and bigger projects, but you pay for Connects to send proposals and the first reviews take work. Use Fiverr reviews as proof.

## Fiverr

**Tagline:** SEO audits and fixes that get small business sites found on Google.

**Profile description**
GB Media Co. is a small agency that helps local and online businesses show up in search. We audit your site, find what is holding it back, and fix it: titles, meta descriptions, headings, schema markup, speed, broken links, and content gaps. You get a plain-English report and a clear to-do list, not a pile of jargon.

## Upwork

**Profile title:** Website SEO Specialist | Audits, Schema, Technical SEO | GB Media Co.
**Hourly rate:** start $40-$60/hr. Add a fixed-price "SEO Audit" project listing at $149-$299.

**Overview**
I run GB Media Co., an agency focused on practical SEO for small businesses. I audit sites, fix technical and on-page issues, add structured data, and plan content around the keywords your customers actually search. You get a written report, a prioritized task list, and fixes done, not just advice. Message me your URL and goals and I will tell you what I would fix first.

**Proposal template**
Hi [Name], I looked at [their site] and noticed [one specific, real issue you checked]. I can fix that and [two more things]. Here is what I would do in the first week: 1) audit, 2) top 10 fixes, 3) report. Want me to send a short sample of what the audit looks like?

**Portfolio:** use 1-2 sample audits of your own site or a friendly business (with permission).

## Videos (about 30-60 seconds each)

1. **Intro:** "Hi, I'm Gabe from GB Media Co. If your site is not showing up on Google, here is what I check first." Show a quick audit screen, name 3 common problems, end with "Send me your URL."
2. **Sample audit walkthrough:** screen recording of an audit report on a demo site: score, top 5 issues, fix list.
3. **Before/after (once you have a real one):** same page, old title and meta vs fixed ones.

Fiverr allows one gig video and Upwork one profile video. Keep each under 75 seconds, good light, clean audio.
