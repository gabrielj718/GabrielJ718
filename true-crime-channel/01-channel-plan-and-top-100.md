## 2. Patterns the data shows (what to copy)

1. **Source footage is the product.** The biggest hits are built on bodycam, interrogation, 911 audio, and trial clips. Your "real photos + interviews + interrogations + animation" concept matches this.
2. **Mid-profile cases with a twist beat famous cases told generically.** Dr Insanity / Explore With Us titles ("Cops Discover…", "Realizes He's Been Caught…") pull 10-39M views on lesser-known cases. Famous cases are a *discovery* engine (people search the name); twist cases are a *recommendation* engine. You want both.
3. **Length 20-45 min, with 1-hour+ for tier-S cases.**
4. **Case waves follow streaming releases.** Avery spiked after *Making a Murderer*; Laci Peterson, Watts, Murdaugh re-spiked with Netflix docs. Watch Netflix/Max/Hulu release calendars and time uploads to them.

## 4. Guardrails (these shape the whole format)

- **Victims: don't generate realistic likenesses.** Since January 2024, YouTube's harassment policy bars content that realistically simulates deceased minors or victims of well-documented violent events narrating their deaths ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/01/08/youtube-cracks-down-ai-generated-content-realistically-simulates-deceased-children-or-victims-of-crimes)). Obviously stylized or animated content is outside the disclosure rule, so a **graphic-novel / illustrated look with faceless or silhouetted figures** protects you and also fits the "Netflix animation" feel.
- **Disclose realistic synthetic content** using YouTube's altered-content toggle; non-disclosure can mean removal or demonetization.
- **Graphic crime-scene photos:** expect age-restriction or limited ads if unblurred. Use court-released exhibits, crop/blur, and put the strongest material behind a warning card. I could not confirm YouTube's current advertiser-friendly text, so check the official Help Center before you finalize.
- **Defamation:** stick to convicted/acquitted facts and court records. Label allegations. Be extra careful with unresolved cases (marked 🔴 below).
- **Child-victim cases:** flagged 👶. Avoid any reenactment of the victim; narrate and use documents only. I left out Bulger, McCann, and Soham deliberately.
- **Mass shootings:** excluded from this list on purpose (perpetrator-glorification and monetization risk).
- **Living people / families:** victims' families often speak publicly; consider a corrections/contact page.
- Not legal advice. Get a media-law (E&O) review before launch.

## 5. How to read the table

- **Tier:** **S** = proven mass appeal / likely to anchor a series; **A** = strong; **B** = solid filler or niche.
- **Flags:** 🎥 strong public video/audio (trial, bodycam, interrogation) · 👶 child victim · ⚠ graphic or sensitive · 🔴 verify current legal status before scripting · 🌍 non-US.
- "Era" = when the crime/trial hit public attention, matched to your 10/15/20-year framing (today = 2026).
